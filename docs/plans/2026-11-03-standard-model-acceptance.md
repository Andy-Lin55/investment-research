# Standard three-statement research candidate

The v17 candidate maps normalized annual industrial statements to forecast categories. The historical balance and annual cash roll-forward must reconcile. Missing borrowing fields remain gaps. Cash equivalents come from the cash-flow statement; the monetary-funds difference remains in other assets. The mapped amounts retain exact source field references, dates, units and a checksum.

The model proposes explicit assumptions. The program projects a base, bull and bear income statement, balance sheet, cash flow and valuation. Costs already include depreciation; the model does not deduct it twice. Share counts and cash issuance or repurchases must agree. Funding shortfalls, invalid asset balances and reconciliation failures suppress valuation in the affected scenario and later years that depend on it. This industrial model does not capture bank accounting, noncash dilution or transaction-level M&A without separate inputs.

The report shows the calculated statements and scenario values in native tables. The linked Excel contains the same calculations and review controls. All projections remain research candidates. The formal model and investment state require human adoption.
